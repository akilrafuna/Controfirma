This page runs the deterministic half of Controfirma — the marker engine from src/rules.js, scoring in your browser, exactly as it does in the product. The explanation layer needs an open model on AMD and a server, so it is not here: watch the video to see the model write explanations and the verifier throw away the ones it cannot source.

One line each. Start a line with caller: or staff: — anything unmarked counts as the caller. Only the caller's words are ever used as evidence.
